\# Autonomous Research Intelligence (ARI)



An AI-powered research intelligence pipeline for discovering, analyzing, ranking, and recommending academic research.



\## Overview



Autonomous Research Intelligence (ARI) is a research-oriented DSAI system that combines natural language processing, semantic embeddings, machine learning, trend analysis, knowledge graphs, and recommendation algorithms into a unified research discovery pipeline.



The system processes an academic research corpus and produces:



\- Research topic clusters

\- Emerging research trends

\- Research intelligence scores

\- Knowledge graph context

\- Semantic research retrieval

\- Intelligence-based ranking

\- Diversity-aware recommendations
